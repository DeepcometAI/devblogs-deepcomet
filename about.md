## About Deepcomet AI

Deepcomet AI is building the next generation of autonomous computing systems. Our mission is to create AI-native infrastructure that self-manages, self-optimizes, and self-heals.

### What We Do

- **Autonomous Systems** — Self-managing kernels and operating systems powered by Large Action Models
- **AI-Native Languages** — Aurelia, a systems programming language with first-class tensor primitives
- **Decentralized AI** — SkyCloud, a peer-to-peer network for collaborative training and inference
- **Intelligent Hardware** — NPU-optimized models for edge and data center deployment

### The Team

Founded by Nehal Aditya, Deepcomet AI brings together researchers and engineers from systems programming, machine learning, and distributed systems backgrounds.
